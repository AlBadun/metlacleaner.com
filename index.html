Careful cleaning

  • Time to rethink

    Quarantine: files are kept for 30 days by default (7, 14 or 30 days free; 60 days or no time limit with Pro).

  • Your files stay on your device

    Scanning and cleaning happen on the device. METLA does not share your list of apps with anyone.

  • Only exact copies

    Finds exact duplicate files (byte for byte) and keeps one original.

  • Honest about what Android allows

    App cache: with usage access, METLA shows how much space it takes and opens the settings where you can clear it (Android does not let one app clear another app’s cache).

Questions and answers

Is Anti-Spy an antivirus?

Activity and Anti-Spy: see how much apps run and which apps have been granted or request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, phone, SMS and storage — with a direct link to change it in settings. Anti-Spy shows access; it is not an antivirus.

Why does METLA need All files access?

On Android 11 and newer, METLA needs All files access to find duplicates of any file type, empty folders and old downloads across your storage, and to move deleted files into Quarantine and back. Without it these features don’t start. METLA never opens other apps’ private folders.

What happens when Quarantine is full?

Its size is limited (1 GB by default, you can change it): when space runs out, METLA warns you and asks before deleting the oldest files for good.

When are analytics sent?

METLA asks for the permissions Android asks you about only when a feature needs them. Analytics and crash reports are sent only after your consent, and you can turn them off in Settings.

Privacy Policy

How does the free trial work?

The 7-day free trial is available once per Google Play account. If you don’t cancel before it ends, the plan price is charged, and the subscription then renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play. All payments go through Google Play.

Does Pro work on my other devices?

Pro works on every device with the same Google Play account; family members get it by signing in to their METLA account.

Which devices and languages?
